# Volunteer Contributor Agreement

> ⚠️ **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language template, not a lawyer-reviewed
> instrument. Before relying on it for a real contributor, have an attorney review it —
> especially because the products it covers are intended to be monetized. Laws on IP
> assignment, "work made for hire," and unpaid contributors vary. Per-contributor fields
> are in brackets; both parties keep a signed copy.

**Living document.** This is the QuickSites copy of a template shared across Point Seven Studio
projects; HiveJournal keeps its own at `docs/contributing/VOLUNTEER_CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md`.
Same owner, same clauses — the only differences are the Project(s) line and the changelog. If you
materially change the terms here, tell the sibling project so the two don't silently diverge on
what a contributor signed.

---

**Between:** Point Seven Studio LLC, of Auburn, Washington ("Project Owner")
**And:** [Contributor's legal name] ("Contributor")
**Effective date:** [date]
**Project(s):** QuickSites and its products and surfaces, including delivered.menu (the "Project")

### 1. Nature of the relationship
The Contributor is a **volunteer**. This is **not** employment, partnership, or a
contract for paid services. No compensation is owed for contributions made under this
agreement unless the parties separately agree in writing. Either party may end the
arrangement at any time, for any reason, with no obligation.

### 2. Assignment of intellectual property
The Contributor agrees that all code, designs, documentation, and other materials they
create for or contribute to the Project ("Contributions") are assigned to the Project
Owner. To the extent any rights do not transfer automatically, the Contributor hereby
irrevocably assigns to Point Seven Studio LLC all worldwide right, title, and interest
in the Contributions, including all copyright and related rights. The Project Owner may
use, modify, sublicense, and commercialize the Contributions without further consent or
compensation.

### 3. Contributor's warranties
The Contributor represents that each Contribution is their original work; that it does
not knowingly infringe anyone else's IP; that they are free to make the assignment in
Section 2 (e.g., it does not conflict with any employer or other agreement they are
bound by); and that they will not include third-party code without disclosing its
license in the pull request.

### 4. Confidentiality
The Contributor will keep non-public information about the Project (unreleased features,
credentials, user data, business plans) confidential, and will not copy, retain, or use
it outside contributing to the Project.

⚠️ **This clause is load-bearing here in a way it is not on a client-only codebase.** QuickSites
holds real business data for real named businesses — client sites, imported listings, customer
records, order history. A contributor may encounter it. See
[`ONBOARDING.md`](ONBOARDING.md) §4 for what that means in practice.

### 5. Credit
The Project Owner will preserve the Contributor's authorship in the git history (commits
under the Contributor's own name/handle), and may, at its discretion, provide a reference
or recommendation reflecting the Contributor's work. Nothing in this section creates an
obligation to provide paid work or employment.

### 6. No warranty / no liability
Contributions are provided "as is." Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or
consequential damages arising from this volunteer arrangement.

### 7. General
This is the entire agreement on its subject and is governed by the laws of the State of
Washington, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. If any part is unenforceable,
the rest remains in effect.
